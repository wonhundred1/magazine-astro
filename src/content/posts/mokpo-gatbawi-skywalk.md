**Hub stay: Mokpo** · **Car:** no · **Mode:** late-afternoon to evening walk from the hotel

Gatbawi and the **Yudal amusement-area skywalk** are **in-city** pieces. You do not need another overnight city. Mokpo tourism’s daytime city-tour spine already strings these stops with Yudalsan and the modern-history halls — useful even if you never board the tour bus.

## Who it is for
- People already based in Mokpo who want a **soft evening** after cable day or arrival day
- Visitors who like sea air more than shopping malls
- Anyone fine with glass floors and light heights

## Place facts (city tourism paraphrase)

| Spot | Public description anchors |
|---|---|
| **Skywalk** | Daeban-dong Yudal leisure area; about **85 m** long, about **15 m** high; majority transparent glass/steel deck over water; views toward **Mokpo Bridge** and often the **marine cable** cabins in the air |
| **Gatbawi + sea pedestrian bridge** | Twin hat-shaped rocks once viewed mainly by boat; a sea **walk bridge** now lets you approach on foot |
| **Pair context** | Same municipal spine as Yudalsan (228 m class hill), Sihwa alley, modern history halls, fish market |

Hours for outdoor decks shift with season and maintenance — check on-site boards and municipal pages the day you go. This piece freezes **no ticket price** because the walk zones are commonly treated as open public waterfront, while any temporary paid zone must be verified live.

## Transit table (from Mokpo stay)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel → skywalk / Yudal waterfront | Taxi (simple) or city bus | Save “목포 스카이워크” / Yudal amusement area pins |
| Skywalk → Gatbawi sea walk | Walk / short hop | Follow waterfront wayfinding; wind can make the glass deck feel colder than the map suggests |
| Optional add: Yudalsan lower paths | Foot | Only if legs remain; do not stack a full summit after a long cable day |
| Return | Taxi / bus | Build buffer before you get too cold to think |

## Hourly outline (dusk)

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 15:30–16:30 | Leave hotel; warm layer on; bathroom stop |
| 16:30–17:30 | Skywalk slow pass; photos without blocking the glass lane |
| 17:30–18:30 | Gatbawi sea bridge loop while residual light holds |
| 18:30–19:30 | Simple dinner via map method; taxi home if wind rises |

If you already rode the cable at sunset, flip the order: shorter Gatbawi walk first, earlier dinner.

## Food method (no invented shops)

| Zone | How to choose |
|---|---|
| Waterfront / Daeban edges | Fewer dense options — verify open pins before you walk hungry |
| Old downtown | Stronger indoor density one taxi hop away |
| Hotel corridor | Best when you are wind-burned |

## Manners
- Glass decks: no running; hold children; non-slip shoes after rain.
- Do not climb rock features or drone without clear local rules.
- Night wind off the water is sharper than daytime apps imply — gloves help in late November.
- **1330** hotline if you need English wayfinding.
