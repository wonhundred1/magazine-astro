**Hub:** Busan · **Car:** no · **Hotel:** optional · **Mode:** full day trip (night sights only with return math)

Gyeongju is one of the **cleanest Gyeongsang day trips** from Busan: short enough to feel doable, long enough that you still need **tables and buffers**. This guide pairs **Bulguksa** with a **downtown heritage walk**, not a five-tomb sprint.

## Who it is for
- First-time visitors who want one temple + one town loop
- Gyopo / family guests who can use Naver Map bus boards
- People who accept temple manners and ticket lines

## Distance honesty

City tourism materials often quote **Busan–Gyeongju bus ~50 minutes class** and **rail ~30 minutes class** on the train segment alone. Treat those as **line times**, not door-to-door. Add terminal access, local buses, and queue time.

## Transit table (pattern — reconfirm same day)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Busan stay → **Nopo** (common coach gate) | Metro | Confirm which terminal sells your Gyeongju coach that day |
| Busan → **Gyeongju** intercity terminal | Coach | High frequency historically; still buy return early on weekends |
| Rail alternative | KTX/ITX-style services into **Gyeongju Station** | Fast on rails; still needs local bus/taxi to Bulguksa |
| Terminal/station → **Bulguksa** | City bus (public notes often cite **10 / 11** class corridors) | Confirm stop name **불국사** in Kakao/Naver same morning |
| Bulguksa → downtown heritage belt | Bus reverse / transfer | Downtown + Wolji/Donggung only if **last return coach** still works |
| Return | Coach/rail reverse | Keep **90+ min** slack after sunset plans |

Gyeongju city EN pages have published example first/last times on some routes (e.g. bus 10 terminal patterns around early morning to ~21:40 class). **Those rot** — screenshot the live board, do not tattoo old minutes.

## Tickets & temple notes

Bulguksa is an active temple/heritage site with **admission rules that change**. Use official temple/city channels the morning you go. This magazine does **not** freeze a ticket price.

## Manners
- Quiet voices on temple paths; dress shoulders/knees respectfully when required.
- No drones, smoking, or off-path shortcuts.
- Night heritage ponds: railings and low light — watch kids.
- 1330 hotline; city tourism desks on official maps.
