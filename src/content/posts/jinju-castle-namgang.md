**Hub:** Busan · **Car:** no · **Hotel:** optional · **Mode:** day trip

Jinju sits on the **western Gyeongnam** side of many Busan travelers’ mental map: fortress stone, **Namgang** curves, and a town scale that rewards walking more than checklist racing.

## Who it is for
- History-minded day-trippers who still want outdoor air
- Visitors who prefer one fortress + river loop over multi-city hopping
- People comfortable with intercity buses and one local transfer

## Distance honesty

Busan transport overviews list **Jinju** from major terminal systems. Expect a **serious half-to-full day**, not a 90-minute round trip. Rail on the Gyeongjeon corridor can work on some dates — compare total time with coaches before you lock a story in your head.

## Transit table (pattern — reconfirm same day)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Busan stay → terminal | Metro | Check whether your ticket leaves from **Nopo** or **Seobu/Sasang**-class gates that day |
| Busan → **Jinju** bus terminal | Intercity coach | Buy return seats early on weekends |
| Terminal → **Jinju Fortress (Jinjuseong)** | City bus / taxi / long walk | Map **진주성**; confirm stop names live |
| Fortress → **Namgang** waterfront | Walk | River paths are the second half of the day |
| Optional museum / old town | Walk | Only if legs remain |
| Return | Reverse | Keep terminal buffer before evening coaches |

## Manners
- Memorial and heritage zones: quiet voices, no climbing restricted walls.
- River paths can be windy and slippery after rain.
- Event days (festivals, races) change crowd control — read city notices.
- 1330; municipal tourism contacts on official pages.
