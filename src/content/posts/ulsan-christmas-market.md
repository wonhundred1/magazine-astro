**Hub:** Busan · **Car:** no · **Hotel:** optional · **Mode:** evening market day-trip (Fri/Sat focused)

VisitKorea describes a **free** Christmas-themed night market at the **Ulsan Grand Park front-gate windmill** area. It is **not** a daily event for the whole month — plan around **Friday/Saturday** cores plus a short Christmas-week expansion.

## Who it is for
- Busan-based travelers who want a **different city night** without Seoul distances
- Families okay with intercity coaches and a local hop to the park
- People who prefer **free entry** (stalls/rides still cost money)

## Distance honesty

Busan ↔ Ulsan is a **real intercity hop**, not a Metro extension. City transport overviews list Ulsan among Busan terminal destinations. Door-to-door with local transfer often lands in a **~1–2 hour class** depending on terminal and traffic — still verify the **last return** before you commit to a 22:00 market close.
