**Hub stay: Gyeongju** · **Car:** no · **Mode:** in-city evening walk · **Not:** a Busan same-day night force

This is the **downtown night spine** for people already sleeping in lodging-rich Gyeongju. It is **not** another Bulguksa checklist — that daytime temple leg lives in a [separate pair](/posts/gyeongju-bulguksa-daytrip/). Tonight is bridges, astronomy stone, and the pond.

## Who it is for
- Visitors with a Gyeongju hotel near the historic core or a short taxi to it
- Photographers who want reflections without a mountain climb
- Families who can finish ticketed Wolji before late closing

## Place facts (municipal paraphrase)

| Spot | Anchors (as of 2026-11-25) |
|---|---|
| **Woljeonggyo (월정교)** | Gyo-dong belt · city tourism: **09:00–22:00**, **free** · gate pavilion exhibit often from **10:00** · free public parking patterns nearby |
| **Cheomseongdae (첨성대)** | Eastern historic district stone observatory · free outdoor viewing class; lighting often runs with city night program — treat as a **short stop**, not a long indoor museum |
| **Donggung & Wolji (동궁과 월지)** | 102 Wonhwa-ro · city tourism: **09:00–22:00**, ticket desk closes **21:30**, year-round open days · adult **3,000** / youth **2,000** / child **1,000** won · online tickets via **gjpass.kr** class systems · free parking pin patterns at Inwang-dong lot |
| **Course logic** | City night-walk materials often order **Woljeonggyo → Cheomseongdae → Wolji** — walk times vary; save the three pins as a pedestrian route |

Lighting end times can differ by season notices — prefer the board at each gate the night you go.

## Transit table (from a Gyeongju stay)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel → first pin (Woljeonggyo) | Walk / short taxi / local bus | If your hotel is Bomun resort belt, budget a taxi rather than a fake “short stroll” |
| Between the three sights | Walk | Use pedestrian directions in Naver/Kakao — not car routes |
| Optional daytime earlier | Separate Bulguksa/Seokguram day | Do **not** stack full temple day + full night walk with exhausted kids |
| Return | Walk/taxi | Wolji exit crowds after 21:00 on weekends |
