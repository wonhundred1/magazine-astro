**Hub city:** Busan · **Style:** day trip · **Car:** not needed · **Overnight:** optional

If you can already read a little Korean on station signs — or you are visiting family in Busan — Maemiseong is one of the cleanest one-day escapes across Geoga Bridge. No rental car. No forced hotel. A long bus, a free stone wall, and a village that still feels lived-in.

## Who this is for
- Overseas Koreans staying with family in Busan
- Travelers comfortable with city buses and Naver/Kakao Map
- People who want a story-heavy stop, not a theme park

## How to go (public transit only)
1. Metro to **Hadan Station** (Line 1).
2. **Bus 2000** toward Geoje / Gohyeon (about every 20-30 minutes; confirm same day).
3. Get off near **Daekeum Intersection**, walk 5-10 minutes into Bokhang village.
4. Return the same way. Saturday afternoon buses toward Busan can fill — keep buffer time.

**Maps:** [Google Maemiseong](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Maemiseong+Geoje) · [Naver 매미성](https://map.naver.com/v5/search/%EB%A7%A4%EB%AF%B8%EC%84%B1)

## What to do in one day
Late morning arrival · arch-window photos · wall and cobble shore (not a swimming beach) · optional continue toward Okpo/Gohyeon for lunch · return before evening crush.

## Good to know
Free entry. Still a personal project opened to the public — take trash home, keep children close on low railings. Confirm temporary notices with Geoje Tourist Information (+82-55-639-4178).
