**Hub:** Busan · **Car:** no · **Hotel:** optional · **Mode:** day trip

This is the longer version of the Maemiseong day trip: rebuilt **tables**, **several maps**, and **nearby cafés** you can actually plug into a one-day loop.

## Who it is for
- Gyopo / family guests based in Busan
- Travelers who can use Naver Map or Kakao Map for buses
- People who want a story stop, not a theme park

## Transit table

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| City → Hadan | Metro Line 1 | Check transfer from your stay |
| Hadan → Daekeum Intersection | Bus **2000** | Often ~20–30 min headway — confirm same day |
| Stop → Maemiseong | Walk 5–10 min | Bokhang village lanes |
| Return | Same | Buffer for busy Saturday afternoons toward Busan |

## Hourly outline

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 09:00–10:00 | Meet at Hadan, board 2000 |
| 10:30–12:30 | Walls, arch window, shore walk (not a swim beach) |
| 12:30–14:00 | Sibang / Bokhang café or simple food |
| 14:00–16:00 | Extra photos or continue toward Okpo on the same corridor |
| 16:00–18:00 | Return buffer |
