From Busan, Geoje is only a bridge away. Skip the usual checklist for a moment and start at **Maemiseong**, a free stone wall rising from a quiet fishing village. The photos look like a European castle. The story is pure Geoje.

## At a glance

- **Address:** 29 Bokhang-gil, Jangmok-myeon, Geoje-si, Gyeongsangnam-do
- **Hours & parking:** free, open 24 hours · public parking nearby
- **Stay:** 1–1.5 hours · best around sunset
- **Tourist info:** Geoje Tourist Information +82-55-639-4178

## Why does this place feel so Geoje?

In September 2003, Typhoon Maemi tore across the south coast. Baek Sun-sam, a ship-design researcher from Geoje's shipyards, lost a seaside field overnight. He was not a trained builder. He began stacking stones so the next storm would not take the land again.

More than twenty years later the work is still unfinished. The wall is introduced as about 13 meters at its highest point and more than 100 meters long (Digital Encyclopedia of Geoje Culture). Geoje City later listed it among the city's Nine Scenic Views.

Geoje builds giant ships from steel. Here, a man who once designed ships built a wall from stone. That is why the place feels local rather than generic Instagram Korea.

## How do you get there from Busan?

- **Bus:** From Hadan Station take bus 2000, get off at Daekeum Intersection, then walk about 5–10 minutes into the village.
- **Car:** Cross Geoga Bridge (toll). Use the public parking lot and avoid driving deep into village alleys.
- **Tip:** Weekend afternoon buses back to Busan can fill up. If day-tripping from Busan, visit Maemiseong on the way into Geoje.

## What should you look for?

- **Arch window photo spot:** Geoga Bridge frames itself through the stone opening.
- **Watchtower view:** Look toward Geoga Bridge and the Gadukdo side of the sea.
- **Cobble beach:** Listen to stones rolling with the waves. Not an official swimming beach.
- **Bokhang village lanes:** People still live here. Walk quietly.

## A gentle overnight route

**Day 1:** Arrive via Geoga Bridge → Maemiseong at late afternoon → stay in Jangmok, Okpo, or Gohyeon.

**Day 2:** Geoje POW Camp Historic Park in Gohyeon (about 40 minutes by car from Maemiseong) → lunch → if time allows, Windy Hill and Sinseondae (about an hour from Gohyeon).

Maemiseong sits at Geoje's northeastern tip. Southern icons are not 20 minutes away.

## Visitor manners

- Take your trash with you.
- Keep children close near low railings.
- Pets: leash and follow on-site guidance.

Transport and lighting schedules change. Confirm with Geoje Tourist Information before you go.
