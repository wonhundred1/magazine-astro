**Hub stay: Busan** · **Car:** no · **Return:** Busan bed · **Mode:** day trip via Hadan

Maemiseong is a **personal construction opened to visitors** — free entry walls and an arch window facing bridge water. It is a story stop, not a theme park. Build the day on **Bus 2000** honesty from Hadan and a cafe method that does not invent rankings.

## Who it is for
- Gyopo / family guests based in Busan
- Travelers who can use Naver Map or Kakao Map for buses
- People who want a short heritage photo stop plus one cafe, not a full island circuit

| Spot | Anchors |

## Transit table (patterns — boards win)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| City → Hadan | Metro Line 1 | Check transfer from your stay |
| Hadan → Daekeum Intersection class | Bus **2000** | Often ~20-30 min headway — confirm same day |
| Stop → Maemiseong | Walk 5-10 min | Village lanes; non-slip in rain |
| Optional Okpo continue | Same corridor | Only if energy and return buffer remain |
| Return | Same | Buffer for busy Saturday afternoons toward Busan |

## Hub stay honesty
- Sleep in **Busan**; Maemiseong is a spoke day.
- Pair [Tongyeong](/posts/tongyeong-dongpirang/) on a **different** day — both are long enough alone.

## Manners
- Free entry; still a personal construction opened to visitors — pack out trash.
- Watch low railings with kids; wind off the water is real in winter.
- Do not climb unfinished edges for a photo.
