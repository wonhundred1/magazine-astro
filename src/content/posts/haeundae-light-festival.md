This is an **event-led night brief** for people already based in Busan. Official Haeundae-gu tourism lists the **13th Haeundae Light Festival** as a free outdoor light walk — not a ticketed arena show.

## Who it is for
- Visitors who want a **same-district evening** without intercity coaches
- Families who prefer free entry and short Metro hops
- People willing to dress for **sea wind after 18:00**

## Hourly outline (lights night)

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 16:30–17:30 | Arrive Haeundae; warm drink; bathroom stop |
| 17:30–18:00 | Position on Gunam-ro or beach edge before peak density |
| 18:00–20:00 | Walk sculptures slowly; photos without blocking lanes |
| 20:00–21:00 | Optional second loop or simple dinner in the station–beach food belt |
| 21:00–22:30 | Quieter second look if wind allows; head metro-ward |

**Opening night (28 Nov):** arrive earlier; fireworks/stage segments compress crowds near the sand.

## Manners
- Free event still needs **lane courtesy** — keep strollers to edges; no drone assumptions.
- Sea wind + long standing: layers, gloves, non-slip shoes.
- Pack out trash; do not climb sculptures.
- Korea Travel Hotline **1330** for English help.
