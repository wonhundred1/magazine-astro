**Hub stay: Daegu** · **Car:** no · **Product:** the **train + village window** · **Not:** Busan same-day force

Buncheon’s Santa station is a **winter rail product**. Sleep in **Daegu** (lodging-rich metro), ride the tourist/theme trains that actually stop at Buncheon, walk the village, ride back. Do not invent a Busan dawn departure.

## Who it is for
- Families and first-timers who want a **clear winter event** with cold-weather honesty
- Travelers willing to **book Korail theme seats early** and reconfirm the morning-of
- People who accept that some on-site experiences stay “announced later” until the foundation posts fees

## Hub stay honesty
- **Daegu** is the metro bed for this product.
- Yeongju can be an alternate lodging-rich gate if you already sleep on the Jungang line — still state it explicitly.
- Do not promise specific sled/rail-bike prices until the foundation posts them.

## Manners
- Theme trains often host guides — keep aisles clear.
- Station platforms get icy; non-slip shoes.
- **1330** · foundation **054-674-3053** class · Korail app for disruptions.
