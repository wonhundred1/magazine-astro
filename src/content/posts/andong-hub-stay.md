**Hub stay: Andong** · **Car:** no · **Airport note:** none required — rail gate · **Not:** Busan same-day sightseeing

Andong is a **lodging-rich inland hub** on the Jungang corridor: station, terminal, downtown river, and the classic **Hahoe** day radius. Stay here. Do not burn daylight on a Busan round trip just to “check” one hanok photo.

## Who it is for
- Travelers entering north Gyeongbuk by KTX-Ieum / ITX / coach
- People building **Hahoe / mask / seowon** legs from a stable hotel base
- Readers who want an **east-rail spine** soft landing before Yeongju or the coast

## Why Andong is the hub
Public tourism courses treat Andong as a **two-day walkable culture city**: municipal museum / folk village belt, **Woryeonggyo** night lights, then a full Hahoe day. Hotels sit near the station–downtown axis. That is the opposite of a thin satellite that forces you back to Daegu every night.

## Arrival table (patterns — reconfirm same day)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Seoul area → **Andong Station** | KTX-Ieum / ITX class on Jungang line | Multi-hour; board times change — Korail app wins |
| Daegu / Dongdaegu area → Andong | Rail or express coach | Useful winter feeder from a Daegu hotel night |
| Busan → Andong | Long coach or rail combo | Treat as a **relocation day**, not a sightseeing day |
| Station / terminal → hotel | Taxi / city bus | Save the hotel pin the night before |
| Andong → **Hahoe** | City tourist bus / local bus / taxi | Full day — see [Hahoe day](/posts/andong-hahoe-day/) |
| Andong → **Yeongju** | Short rail hop | Optional next hub on the same spine |

## What to do from this hub (index)
1. **Arrival evening:** Woryeonggyo + riverside lights (free outdoor walk; dress for wind)
2. **Full day:** [Hahoe Village](/posts/andong-hahoe-day/) (+ mask museum belt when open)
3. **Optional culture:** municipal museum / folk village pins via map search — verify hours morning-of
4. **Next hub:** Yeongju Museom / Buseoksa only with honest bus math ([Yeongju piece](/posts/yeongju-museom-village/))

| Time | Plan |

| Dusk | Walk or short taxi to Woryeonggyo belt |

## Hub stay honesty
- Sleep **in Andong** when Andong is the product.
- Daegu is a fine feeder city; it is not a substitute overnight if your mornings are Hahoe queues.
- Daejeon can be a long-day gate in other pieces — this guide assumes you already chose Andong as base.

## Manners
- Bridge and reservoir parks stay quiet after late evening.
- **1330** for English help; hotel front desks usually know the Hahoe bus stop name of the week.
