**Hub stay: Gwangju** (default lodging-rich) · optional **Suncheon** if already based there · **Not:** Busan same-day force

Gokseong’s **Seomjin Train Village** is a family-forward Jeolla day: park walk, optional steam hop, optional rail-bike — all with **separate tickets**. Sleep in a real hub; treat Gokseong as the attraction, not a thin overnight default.

## Who it is for
- Families who want a train-themed outdoor day without inventing mountain itineraries
- Travelers OK with **ticket stacking** (park + ride + bike)
- People who will check **winter steam cancellations** the morning they go

## Place facts (county tourism paraphrase)

| Item | Public listing (as of materials checked 2026-11-25) |
|---|---|
| **Park admission** | Adult **5,000** won · child/senior **4,500** · group discounts on county table · free bands for residents/disabled/patriots with ID · desks **061-362-7461** (main) / **061-362-8635** (north) |
| **What park ticket covers** | Rose park / insect hall / animal farm class areas — **not** steam or rail-bike |
| **Steam train (village ⇔ Gajeong)** | Multiple daily round patterns; **first morning slot often suspended Nov–Mar** on county table — midday slots still listed year-round · adult round seat band **~10,000** won class (2025-08 fee notice on page) · ops **061-363-6174 / 9900** |
| **Village rail-bike** | Loop inside village · **~10,000** won / 4-seat car class · roughly **09:00–17:30** daily pattern · on-site purchase |
| **Seomjin rail-bike (Gajeong)** | Separate product toward Bongjo turn · long operating window with midday maintenance gap — **061-362-7717** |
| **Inquiry** | Always re-open the county fee page morning-of; seasonal suspensions hit steam first |

## Transit table (patterns — boards win)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gwangju / Suncheon hotel → terminal | Taxi / city bus | Save terminal + Train Village pins |
| Hub → **Gokseong** | Intercity bus / rail combo | Multi-leg common; **boards beat blogs** |
| Gokseong town → Train Village | Local bus / taxi | Do not assume a one-block walk from every drop |
| Inside park | Walk + optional paid rides | Stack at most **one** major paid ride with kids |
| Return | Reverse | Build early return; rural last buses thin out |

## Hourly outline (train-forward day)

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| Morning | Arrive village; park ticket; bathroom |
| Late morning | Park walk + **one** steam or rail-bike (check winter steam board) |
| Midday | Lunch via open pins near village / town |
| Early afternoon | Second light walk **or** gift shop — not a second expensive ride by default |
| Mid afternoon | Begin return to hub |

## Food method (no invented shops)

| Zone | How to choose |
|---|---|
| Inside/edge of village | Tourist snacks; verify open shutters |
| Gokseong-eup | Better sit-down density for a real meal |
| Back at hub | Gwangju/Suncheon dinner if you leave early |

County pages mention river-fish and local produce categories — choose by **open kitchens**, not rankings.

## Hub stay honesty
- Prefer **Gwangju** lodging-rich base for most readers.
- **Suncheon** works if you are already on a Suncheon Bay cluster.
- Do not force Busan same-day for a train-park day.

## Manners
- Keep kids clear of tracks except boarded rides.
- Animal farm areas: no feeding unless staffed programs say so.
- **1330** for English help; park desks above for Korean-first detail.
