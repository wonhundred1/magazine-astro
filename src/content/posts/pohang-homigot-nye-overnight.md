**Hub stay: Pohang** · **Car:** no · **Return:** Pohang bed after sunrise · **Not:** Busan same-night round-trip

Homigot’s year-end sunrise festival is a **free** overnight on the peninsula’s east tip. VisitKorea lists **2026-12-31 ~ 2027-01-01**, venue **Homigot Sunrise Square**, organizer Pohang Cultural Foundation, inquiry **+82-54-289-7852**. Program window class often runs afternoon into next-morning sunrise — **reconfirm the year’s stage sheet**. Sleep in **Pohang**; do not treat this as a Busan same-night dash.

For a non-festival winter day from Busan, see the earlier [Homigot & Guryongpo day](/posts/pohang-homigot-winter/).

## Who it is for
- Travelers who already booked a **Pohang** hotel or guesthouse for Dec 30–Jan 1
- People willing to stand outside after midnight in real wind
- Families who want free tteokguk / kite / balloon style programs when the foundation runs them — without inventing booth names

## Transit table (patterns — boards win)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |

| Arrive Pohang | KTX / ITX / coach | Station or terminal → hotel first |

| Homigot → hotel after sunrise | Reverse bus / taxi | Build slack; wind delays |
| Pohang ↔ Busan | Intercity coach / rail | Fine the **day after**; not the same night as the festival |

1. Pohang hotel check-in and early dinner  
2. Homigot Sunrise Square programs / toilets  
3. Hands of Harmony rail for sunrise  
4. Reverse to hotel after dawn — Guryongpo only on another day

## Overnight honesty
- **Pohang lodging is the product** for NYE — book early; empty rooms vanish.
- Busan same-night return after sunrise is a fatigue trap and thin on last coaches.
- Pair Guryongpo or Yeongildae as a **separate** daylight day.

## Manners
- Working coast + memorial space: quiet after programs wind down.
- Share sculpture rails; no drones where signs ban them.
- Free food lines: one portion etiquette.
- **1330**; foundation **054-289-7852**.
