**Hub stay: Gwangju** (lodging-rich) · **Car:** no · **Return:** Gwangju bed · **Event:** 2026-12-18 ~ 12-31

Hampyeong’s winter light festival is free and short: foundation + VisitKorea list **2026-12-18 (Fri) ~ 2026-12-31 (Thu)** at **Hampyeong Expo Park and town streets**, theme lights / media art / parade class, inquiry **061-320-2247**. Operating window **16:00–21:00**; **night scenery maintained through 2027-01-17** after the formal festival. Entry **free**.

## Who it is for
- Gwangju-based travelers who want a Jeolla light night without Busan distance
- Families OK with early dinner + 16:00 park entry
- People who will **check the last train/bus before 21:00** (rail last runs can be earlier than festival end)

## Overnight honesty
- **Gwangju** stay is the default.
- Hampyeong lodging is thin — only with a confirmed booking if you want post-21:00 lights into January scenery nights.
- Mokpo is a longer alternate; do not force both Mokpo cable and Hampyeong lights same evening.

## Manners
- Free event: no blocking emergency paths with tripods.
- Winter wind on open expo lawns — layers.
- **1330**; foundation **061-320-2247**.
