**Hub:** Busan · **Car:** no · **Hotel:** optional (sunrise people: yes) · **Mode:** long east-coast day

Homigot is the **eastern cape story**: wind, sea hand sculpture, and winter light. Pair it with **Guryongpo** only if your bus math stays honest. This is **not** a casual half-day from Busan.

## Who it is for
- Travelers who like coast + simple monuments more than shopping malls
- Winter food curious visitors (gwamegi season) who still verify shops on site
- People preparing for **year-end sunrise** crowds (separate overnight plan)

## Distance honesty

Busan terminal lists include **Pohang**. After the coach you still need **city buses** toward Guryongpo and onward to Homigot. Public notes describe **200 / 200-1** class services toward Guryongpo with further local links to Homigot — headways and last buses **must** be rechecked the morning you travel.

## Transit table (pattern — reconfirm same day)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Busan stay → terminal | Metro | Compare Nopo vs other gates for Pohang coaches that day |
| Busan → **Pohang** | Intercity bus (rail alternative exists on some itineraries) | Multi-hour class door-to-door once local legs count |
| Pohang → **Guryongpo** | City bus (**200 / 200-1** patterns in public notes) | Confirm stop **구룡포** live |
| Guryongpo → **Homigot** | Local bus / short hop | Do not assume high frequency in winter evenings |
| Return | Full reverse | Homigot late light is beautiful and **dangerous for last-bus math** |
