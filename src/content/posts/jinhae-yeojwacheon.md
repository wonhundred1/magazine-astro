**Hub city:** Busan · **Often about 1 hour** · **Car:** not needed

Jinhae is famous for April cherry blossoms, but the canal town also works off-season as a soft day trip from Busan — fewer queues, slower coffee, same public bus logic.

## Who this is for
- Families who want flat walking more than mountain stairs
- Visitors chasing Gunhangje photos *or* a quiet shoulder-season stroll
- Gyopo travelers based in Busan for a short stay

## How to go
1. Metro Line 2 to **Sasang** → **Seobu terminal**.
2. Bus toward **Jinhae (진해)** — read the destination board carefully.
3. Local move to **Yeojwacheon (여좌천)**.
4. Same-day return to Sasang.

**Maps:** [Google Yeojwacheon](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Yeojwacheon+Jinhae) · [Naver 여좌천](https://map.naver.com/v5/search/%EC%97%AC%EC%A2%8C%EC%B2%9C)

## Seasonal honesty
Late March–early April is beautiful and crowded. Other months are calmer and some shops quieter. Some corridor buses run limited daily frequencies — confirm the same morning.
