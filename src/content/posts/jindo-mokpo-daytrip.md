Jindo is the classic **west-Jeolla day radius** from a Mokpo hotel: bridge gateway, **Uldolmok** current, tower overlook, optional arts legs when live. Sleep in Mokpo. Do not invent a Busan round trip.

## Who it is for
- Travelers already on a [Mokpo hub stay](/posts/mokpo-hub-stay/)
- History/landscape walkers who accept **long local buses** or a weekend package bus
- People who will reconfirm **last return coaches** before they leave the hotel

## Hourly outline (self-guide winter day)

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| Morning | Leave Mokpo early; coach toward Jindo / Nokjin |
| Late morning | Bridge viewpoint walk + tower block (watch **17:00** winter close) |
| Midday | Simple lunch via open pins in Jindo-eup or near Nokjin |
| Early afternoon | One culture stop **or** rest — not both if buses are thin |
| Mid–late afternoon | Begin return to Mokpo with daylight buffer |

Weekend tour riders: follow the operator sheet, not this hourly table.

## Hub stay honesty
- **Mokpo** is the lodging-rich base ([hub piece](/posts/mokpo-hub-stay/)).
- Jindo overnight only if you intentionally booked real lodging — do not invent inns here.
- Pair with [Mokpo marine cable](/posts/mokpo-marine-cable-gohado/) or [Gatbawi evening](/posts/mokpo-gatbawi-skywalk/) on other days, not the same day.

## Manners
- Coastal wind at the strait — layers, non-slip shoes, secure hats.
- Tower and battle memorials are solemn sites; keep music low.
- **1330** for English help; tower desk **061-542-0990**.
