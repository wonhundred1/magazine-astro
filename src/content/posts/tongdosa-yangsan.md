**Hub stay: Busan** · **Car:** no · **Return:** Busan bed · **Mode:** Nopo half-day or soft full day

Tongdosa is the **pine-path temple** closest to Busan north-terminal logic: UNESCO **Sansa** cluster member, Buddha-relic tradition, long approach walk. Official pages list **temple entry free since 2023-05-04**, grounds open **08:30-17:30** class (dawn chant participants earlier). Parking still fees if you arrive by car — this guide assumes bus.

## Who it is for
- Busan-based walkers who want a temple without a full Gyeongju day
- People OK with a long approach path and hall crowds on weekends
- Visitors who will treat the **museum annex** as optional (hours/construction notes shift)

## Hub stay honesty
- Sleep in **Busan**; Tongdosa is a spoke, not a new overnight.
- Do not stack Tongdosa + full Gimhae museum + night market on short winter light.

## Manners
- Cover shoulders/knees in halls; expect crowds on Buddha Birthday and sunny weekends.
- No loud speakerphone on the approach path.
- Pack out snacks; temple courts are not picnic grounds unless marked.
- Dawn chant is a practice window — join only with posted guidance.
