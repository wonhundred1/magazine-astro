**Hub:** Busan · **Car:** no · **Hotel:** **recommended** · **Mode:** long day trip or 1 night

Jeonju is **Jeolla gravity**, not a casual Busan suburb hop. Frame it as a **long day** or a **relaxed overnight**. Anything else becomes a terminal sport.

## Who it is for
- Travelers who want alley atmosphere more than a single photo spot
- Visitors willing to recheck express-bus seats both ways
- People who can keep voices down in a **lived-in village**

## Distance honesty

Busan city transport pages list **Jeonju** among express/intercity destinations (often via **Nopo**-class gates). Door-to-door commonly lands in a **multi-hour** band once you add metro, boarding, and the walk into the village. Rail itineraries from Busan may involve **transfers** — never assume a one-seat KTX the way Seoul–Jeonju marketing sometimes reads.

## Transit table (pattern — reconfirm same day)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Busan stay → **Nopo Express Bus Terminal** | Metro | Classic long-haul gate; verify platform the night before |
| Nopo → **Jeonju Express Bus Terminal** | Express coach | Buy **return** early on weekends and kimchi-season peaks |
| Terminal → **Hanok Village / Jeondong** area | City bus or taxi | Official village maps mark approaches; Kakao/Naver destination **전주한옥마을** |
| Village loop | Walk | Alleys are the product — schedule idle time |
| Optional museums / walls | Local bus | Only if overnight or very early start |
| Return | Reverse | Last coaches matter more than Instagram sunsets |

## Hourly outline (long day)

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 06:30–07:30 | Leave Nopo on an early coach |
| 10:30–11:30 | Arrive Jeonju; transfer toward village |
| 11:30–15:30 | Alleys, Jeondong area exterior views, slow lunch |
| 15:30–16:30 | Buffer to terminal |
| 16:30–20:30 | Return coach + Busan metro |

**Overnight upgrade:** evening lanes without racing the last bus; morning second loop before checkout.

## Manners
- Hanok village is residential + commercial — **quiet nights**, no blocking gates.
- Drones and tripods often restricted; follow signs.
- Weekends are dense; weekdays feel more like a neighborhood.
- 1330; city/village info desks on official maps.
