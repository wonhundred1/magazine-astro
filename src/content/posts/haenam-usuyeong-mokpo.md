**Hub stay: Mokpo** · **Car:** no · **Return:** Mokpo bed · **Not:** Busan same-day sightseeing

Usuyeong is the **Haenam-side Myeongnyang** day: free outdoor park, battle memorials, optional **maritime cable** over Uldolmok. Sleep in Mokpo. Pair with the [Jindo day](/posts/jindo-mokpo-daytrip/) on a different date — do not stack both strait legs plus late coaches in one winter afternoon.

## Who it is for
- Travelers already on a [Mokpo hub stay](/posts/mokpo-hub-stay/)
- History walkers who accept **thin rural coaches** and wind
- People who will phone the cable line on windy days before leaving the hotel

## Place facts (official paraphrase — checked 2026-11-26)

| Spot | Anchors |
|---|---|
| **Usuyeong Tourist Site** | Munnae-myeon, Haenam · VisitKorea / county tourism: Myeongnyang memorial park · **09:00–18:00** class day window · **free entry** (open-tourism notes) · desk patterns **061-530-5541** / tourism **061-532-1330** |
| **Myeongnyang naval history exhibition** | Turtle-ship-shaped museum block inside the park belt — elevators/ramps noted on open-tourism pages |
| **Skywalk / overlook** | County lists a skywalk inside the Usuyeong cluster — treat as outdoor wind exposure |
| **Myeongnyang Maritime Cable Car** | Haenam station: **Gwangwang leisure-ro 12-20**, Munnae · inquiry **061-535-9900** · ~1 km over Uldolmok · public fare boards commonly list **general cabin adult round-trip ~₩15,000** / crystal higher · weekday open often **10:00–18:00** (ticket cut ~**17:30**); weekend open often **09:30** — **operator page wins; wind stops service** |
| **Jindo station side** | Cable can drop you near the Jindo Tower belt — only useful if you planned a one-way and a separate return coach |

## Transit table (patterns — boards win)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mokpo hotel → **Mokpo Bus Terminal** | Taxi / city bus | Save the terminal pin; morning departure |
| Mokpo → **Haenam-eup** | Intercity coach | Public pattern tables often show **~7/day**, first ~**07:25**, last ~**20:00**, ~**₩9,500** class — **reconfirm terminal board** |
| Mokpo → **Usuyeong** direct | Rural/intercity pattern | Some boards list **Usuyeong** as a stop on Jindo-direction legs (first ~**06:30** class) — ask the window, do not invent a clock time |
| Haenam-eup → Usuyeong | Local bus / taxi | Build slack; winter daylight is short |
| Cable Haenam ↔ Jindo | Operator cabins | Round-trip keeps the car/coach logic simple if you started on Haenam |
| Return → Mokpo | Reverse coach | Leave buffer before dark; west wind delays happen |

## Hourly outline (winter day from Mokpo)

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| Early morning | Coach toward Haenam / Usuyeong; layers + non-slip shoes |
| Late morning | Free park loop: memorials, exhibition, overlook |
| Midday | Simple lunch via open pins near Usuyeong or Haenam-eup |
| Early afternoon | Optional cable **only if wind allows** and ticket line is open |
| Mid–late afternoon | Begin return to Mokpo with daylight buffer |

Skip the cable if the queue will kill your last coach — the free park already carries the history story.

## Hub stay honesty
- **Mokpo** is the lodging-rich base ([hub piece](/posts/mokpo-hub-stay/)).
- Haenam overnight only with a real booking — do not invent inns here.
- Same-week pairings: [Mokpo marine cable](/posts/mokpo-marine-cable-gohado/), [Gatbawi evening](/posts/mokpo-gatbawi-skywalk/), [Jindo day](/posts/jindo-mokpo-daytrip/) — **one deep leg per day**.

## Manners
- Memorial park: keep music low; treat battle sites as solemn.
- Cable cabins: no food inside; secure hats in wind.
- **1330** English help · cable **061-535-9900** · Usuyeong desk **061-530-5541**.
