**Hub city:** Busan · **Often under an hour** · **Easiest of this series**

If longer intercity days feel intimidating, start with Gimhae. The light rail makes it feel closer to a metro outing than a terminal mission — good for mixed-age family groups.

## Who this is for
- First public-transit day trip from a Busan base
- Museum and outdoor heritage without mountain hiking
- Travelers who may connect to Gimhae Airport later

## How to go
1. Ride the **Busan-Gimhae Light Rail (BGL)** from a convenient Busan junction (check Naver Map from your hotel).
2. Continue into central Gimhae.
3. Walk or short local hop to the **royal tumuli / museum cluster**.
4. BGL back to Busan before dinner.

**Maps:** [Google Gimhae Royal Tombs](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Gimhae+Royal+Tombs) · [Naver 수로왕릉](https://map.naver.com/v5/search/%EA%B9%80%ED%95%B4%20%EC%88%98%EB%A1%9C%EC%99%95%EB%A6%89)

## One-day outline
Late morning outbound · tombs + museum · lunch near stations · optional cafe · light-rail home.

## Notes
Heritage-heavy, not a beach day. Check museum closed weekdays. Pair with a Busan coastal evening if you want contrast.
