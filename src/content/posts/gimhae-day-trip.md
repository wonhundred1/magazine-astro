**Hub stay: Busan** · **Car:** no · **Return:** Busan bed · **Mode:** BGL day trip

Gimhae is the **easiest culture day** from a Busan hotel: light rail, free royal-tomb park, free national museum. No coach lottery. Winter daylight still fits a tombs + museum loop if you leave mid-morning.

## Who it is for
- First-week Busan stays who want a low-stress half day
- Families who prefer elevators and paved paths over mountain temples
- Travelers who will still check **Monday museum closure** before locking the plan

## Place facts (official paraphrase — checked 2026-11-27)

| Spot | Anchors |
|---|---|
| **Suro Royal Tomb** | Gimhae tourism: Wangneung-gil 26 class · **free entry** · winter (Nov-Feb) **09:00-18:00**; shoulder/summer longer evening windows on portal tables · desk **055-332-1094** |
| **Gimhae National Museum** | Official: permanent **09:00-18:00**, last entry **30 min before close** · **closed Mondays** (+ New Year / Seollal / Chuseok class) · **free** (special shows may fee) · **055-320-6800** · Gaya archaeology focus |
| **BGL logic** | Airport-adjacent light rail helps some PUS arrivals; city hotels use metro to BGL junction |
| **Optional cluster** | Daeseong-dong tumuli / folk museum pins via map — only if energy remains |

## Transit table (patterns — boards win)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Stay → BGL junction | Metro / walk | Naver/Kakao shortest path from your hotel |
| BGL → Gimhae stops | Light rail | Aim tomb / museum stations; exit names change less than bus stops |
| Station → tombs | Walk ~5-10 min class | Royal tomb park is compact |
| Tombs → National Museum | Walk / short hop | Cluster rather than zigzag across the city |
| Return | BGL | Softest evening return in the Busan-hub set |

## Hourly outline

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 10:00-10:30 | BGL out from Busan junction |
| 11:00-12:30 | Suro tomb park loop (gates, stone figures, quiet paths) |
| 12:30-13:30 | Open-now lunch near tomb/museum belt |
| 13:30-15:30 | National Museum permanent halls (skip if Monday) |
| 15:30- | BGL return; optional cafe only if energy remains |

## Stops (order that usually works)
1. **Royal tomb park** first while outdoor light is kinder
2. **Simple meal** with a saved pin
3. **National Museum** second so closing time is the hard stop
4. Optional third heritage pin only after checking last BGL comfort

## Food method (no invented shops)

| Zone | How to choose |
|---|---|
| Tomb / museum belt | Three **Open now** pins; thin listings stay off sales sheets until road address verified |
| Station exits | Faster bowls if museum line runs long |
| Back in Busan | Default dinner base — do not force a late Gimhae hop |

## Hub stay honesty
- Sleep in **Busan**; this is a local light-rail day, not a relocation.
- Pair [Tongdosa](/posts/tongdosa-yangsan/) on another day — temple + museum same afternoon is a forced march.

## Manners
- Museum: no food inside halls; phones silent; no flash/tripod commercial shoots without permit (museum rules).
- Tomb park is a memorial landscape — keep voices low near ceremonial buildings.
- **1330** for English help if signs confuse.
