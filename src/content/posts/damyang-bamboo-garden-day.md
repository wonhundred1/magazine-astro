**Hub stay: Gwangju** · **Car:** no · **Day radius:** Damyang-eup · **Not:** a forced Busan hop

Damyang is the classic **Jeolla forest day** from a lodging-rich Gwangju hotel: **Juknokwon** bamboo walks, the **Metasequoia** road belt, and short hops inside the town. Stay in Gwangju unless you intentionally booked Damyang lodging.

## Who it is for
- Travelers who want green quiet without a mountain scramble
- Families OK with ticket gates and winter last-admission clocks
- Map-literate visitors who can ride Gwangju–Damyang buses both ways

## Place facts (official paraphrase)

| Spot | Anchors (as of 2026-11-25) |
|---|---|
| **Juknokwon (죽녹원)** | 119 Jungnogwon-ro, Damyang-eup · official/VisitKorea: **Mar–Oct 09:00–19:00** (last in **18:00**), **Nov–Feb 09:00–18:00** (last in **17:30**), no regular closed day · adult **3,000** / teen **1,500** / child **1,000** won · free bands for preschoolers & 65+ with ID · +82-61-380-2680 · juknokwon.go.kr |
| **Trail class** | Multi-theme bamboo paths (order of **~2.2–2.4 km** total on public materials) — pick 1–2 loops, not a forced full circuit in winter light |
| **Metasequoia Road** | Damyang-eup metasequoia belt · open-year landscape road; some managed sections use timed entry — **confirm board day-of** · tourism inquiry patterns **061-380-3149** |
| **Pair stops** | Gwanbangjerim riverside trees; Damyang Hyanggyo edge — walkable when energy remains |
| **Rules** | No smoking, no outside cooking, no bamboo graffiti/shoot picking; wheelchair loan at rear gate — official site steers mobility users toward flatter Siga Culture Village zones |

## Transit table (patterns — boards win)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gwangju hotel → **U-Square / Gwangju Bus Terminal** | Metro/taxi/city bus | Save the terminal pin the night before |
| Gwangju → **Damyang Terminal** | Intercity / rural seat bus (**311 class** stops at Juknokwon on many runs) | Municipal traffic pages list frequent Gwangju–Damyang intervals; **day-of BIS/boards** win |
| Terminal / bus stop → Juknokwon | Short walk or remaining bus stop | Front gate **Jungnogwon-ro 119** |
| Juknokwon → Metasequoia belt | Local hop / taxi | Do not assume one continuous free walk between both in winter cold |
| Return → Gwangju | Reverse | Watch last evening buses; build dinner buffer in Gwangju |

Exact minute tables change — open Damyang BIS / terminal boards the morning you go rather than freezing one PDF here.

## Hourly outline (winter bamboo day)

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| Morning | Gwangju → Damyang; arrive before late-morning crowds |
| Late morning | Juknokwon ticket + one theme loop (watch **17:30** last entry) |
| Midday | Lunch via open pins in Damyang-eup |
| Early afternoon | Metasequoia belt **or** Gwanbangjerim — pick one |
| Mid afternoon | Begin return to Gwangju with daylight buffer |

## Food method (no invented shops)

| Zone | How to choose |
|---|---|
| Damyang-eup near terminal | High turnover; bamboo-rice and tteokgalbi categories appear on municipal food pages — pick open kitchens day-of |
| Near Juknokwon | Tourist-lane cafes; verify hours before you climb out cold |
| Back in Gwangju | Often the better dinner density |

We do **not** invent a “must bamboo cafe” list.

## Hub stay honesty
- **Gwangju** is lodging-rich — stay there and day-trip.
- Damyang overnight only with a real booking; thin dates push you back to Gwangju.
- Pair later with Jeonju or Suncheon legs as separate days — not the same day.

## Manners
- Bamboo is a living forest park — stay on paths; no carving stems.
- Winter paths can be damp; non-slip shoes.
- **1330** for English help; Juknokwon **061-380-2680**.
