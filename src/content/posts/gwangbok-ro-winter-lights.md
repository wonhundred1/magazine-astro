**Hub stay: Busan** · **Car:** no · **Hotel:** stay in Busan · **Mode:** evening walk (Nampo / Jung-gu)

This is the **original downtown** winter-lights spine — not Haeundae beach wind, not Seomyeon mall arches. Jung-gu frames **Gwangbok-ro / Gwangbokjungang-ro** as a free street festival that closes the year.

## Who it is for
- People already sleeping in Busan who want a **Nampo evening** without tickets
- Families who prefer Metro Line 1 exits over long taxi loops
- Visitors pairing daytime Jagalchi / BIFF Square with night trees

Detail programs after the ceremony often roll out on **Jung-gu SNS** first — check the week you walk.

## Manners
- Free event still needs lane courtesy — no blocking shop doors for tripods.
- Wet tile after rain is slick under tree reflections.
- Pack out trash; keep portable speakers off.
- **1330** for English help.
