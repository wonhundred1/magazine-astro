**Hub stay: Mokpo** (in-city) · **Car:** no · **Mode:** half-day to full day from the hotel

Public operator and city materials describe Korea’s **longest marine cable** run: about **3.23 km**, **North Port → Yudalsan bend → Gohado** (a bent line over city hill and open water).

## Who it is for
- Travelers already sleeping in Mokpo
- Sunset chasers who can leave a **last-cabin buffer**
- Families OK with heights (crystal-floor cabins are optional, not required)

## Verified ticket facts (operator site, 2026-11-25 morning)

| Item | Public listing |

## Transit table (from a Mokpo hotel)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel → North Port station | City bus / taxi | Kakao/Naver day-of; save North Port cable station pin |
| North Port ↔ Yudalsan ↔ Gohado | Marine cable | Segment choice; operator marketing often cites a **~40 min** class round experience — **field time wins** |
| Gohado station → observatory walk | Foot | VisitKorea-style notes often put the walk in a **10–15 min** class — wear shoes for wind |
| Return | Cable reverse + city hop | Do **not** cut sunset to the absolute last cabin |

## Manners & safety
- Operator warns of **early close without long notice** in bad weather — have a plan B walk.
- Platform wind; stroller/wheelchair notes exist for wide-door cabins — still ask staff.
- Crystal cabin is a surcharge choice, not a moral requirement.
- Pack layers; sea glare is real even in November.
