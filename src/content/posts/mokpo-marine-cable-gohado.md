**Hub stay: Mokpo** (in-city) · **Car:** no · **Mode:** half-day to full day from hotel · **Info:** +82-61-244-2600 · [mmcablecar.com](http://www.mmcablecar.com/)

Operator and VisitKorea describe one of Korea’s **longest marine cable** runs (**~3.23 km**, North Port ↔ Yudalsan ↔ Gohado; round trip often ~40 minutes riding). Weather can stop service without long notice.

## Who it is for
- Travelers already sleeping in Mokpo  
- Sunset plans with **last-ticket buffers**  
- Families OK with heights (crystal floor cabins optional)  

## Fares (operator page — reconfirm day-of)

| Cabin | Round-trip adult | Round-trip child | One-way adult | One-way child |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 24,000 KRW | 18,000 KRW | 19,000 KRW | 13,000 KRW |
| Crystal | 29,000 KRW | 23,000 KRW | 22,000 KRW | 16,000 KRW |

- Child band on operator pages: roughly age 3 (36 months) through elementary; adult from middle-school age up — **check board**.  
- Under 36 months: free (with proof rules).  
- Discounts (senior, disability, Mokpo resident, groups 20+) exist — bring ID; no stacking promos.  
- Parking: often **3 h free** with ride QR, then hourly fee (operator board).  

## Hours (operator / VisitKorea pattern)

| Season | Mon–Thu | Fri–Sun & holidays |
|---|---|---|
| Summer pattern | 09:00–20:00 | 09:00–21:00 |
| **Winter pattern (Nov–Feb)** | 09:00–19:00 | 09:00–20:00 |

**Last ticketing: 1 hour before close.** Always open the operator homepage popup for “today’s” hours.

## Transit from Mokpo stay

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel → **North Port** station | City bus / taxi | North Port has parking + tickets; easiest start |
| North Port → Yudalsan → Gohado | Marine cable | Yudalsan mid station: **no car access**; hiking approach if you start there |
| Gohado station → **observatory** | Walk ~10–15 min | Free viewpoint; public notes often **09:00–18:00** — finishes **before** late cable |
| Return | Cable reverse | Do not cut sunset to the last cabin |
