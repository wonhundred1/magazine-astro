**Hub stay: Yeongju** · **Car:** no · **Return:** Yeongju bed · **Mode:** slow village walk

Museom is the **water-island village** south of Yeongju: a free outdoor heritage walk famous for the **narrow single-log bridge**. Stay in Yeongju. Bus math is thin — plan one deep stop, not a fantasy three-temple sprint.

## Who it is for
- Photographers and slow walkers who accept **few daily buses**
- Travelers already on the Jungang / Yeongju rail gate (including Santa-train feeders)
- Families who will hold kids’ hands on a **railless** narrow bridge

## Hub stay honesty
- **Yeongju** is lodging-capable for this leg; Andong is the sibling hub for Hahoe ([Andong hub](/posts/andong-hub-stay/)).
- Seonbi-chon construction or partial closures happen — check Yeongju notices before promising a combo ticket day.
- Santa-train travelers: Museom is a **different day** from Buncheon unless you truly hold both seat maps.

## Manners
- Residents live here — no entering unmarked yards.
- Bridge: one-way courtesy, no running, no drones over houses.
- **1330** for English help.
