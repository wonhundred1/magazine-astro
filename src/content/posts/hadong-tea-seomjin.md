**Hub stay: Hadong** if lodging works · else **Jinju** (nearest strong base) · **Not** a forced Busan same-day

Hadong sits on the **Namhae inland belt** — wild tea slopes, **Hwagae**, and the **Seomjin** river plain under Jirisan’s fringe. Prefer sleeping close. A long Busan round trip eats the daylight you came for.

## Who it is for
- Slow travelers who want tea landscape more than checklist temples
- People OK with fewer English signs and more map literacy
- Anyone pairing later **Jirisan foothill** days from a nearer bed

## Place facts (official / VisitKorea paraphrase)

| Spot | Anchors |
|---|---|
| **Hadong Tea Cultural Center / Tea Museum** | 571-25 Ssanggye-ro, Hwagae-myeon · VisitKorea: **Mar–Oct 10:00–18:00**, **Nov–Feb 10:00–17:00**, closed Mondays + 1 Jan + Seollal/Chuseok days · +82-55-884-2955 · hadongteamuseum.org |
| **Hwagae Market** | Traditional market at Seomjin/Hwagae stream meeting · VisitKorea hours class **09:00–18:00**, daily · tea shops + regional produce · + pins on tour.hadong.go.kr |
| **Tea field / Hwagae dongcheon belt** | Municipal “ten views” include wild tea fields and Seomjin lookouts — treat as **landscape walks**, not a single ticket gate |
| **Hadong Pine Forest Park** | Seomjin + pine + sand edge near Hadong-eup (VisitKorea nearby list) — open-year orientation; confirm any local notices |
| **Inquiry** | Hadong culture/tourism **055-880-2375** · tourist call center **1588-3186** · Hwagae info patterns around **055-883-5722** |

Tea **experience programs** (etiquette, harvest-season picking) are often **reservation-only** — do not assume walk-in English classes in November.

## Transit table (patterns — boards win)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jinju / Suncheon / western Busan belt → **Hadong** | Intercity bus | Multi-leg possible; **terminal boards** beat blog memories |
| Hadong Terminal → Hwagae / tea museum belt | Local bus / taxi | Save Hwagae + museum pins morning-of; winter daylight is short |
| Optional river plain (pine forest / town) | Local hop | Do this **or** Hwagae deep — stacking both without a car is ambitious |
| Return | Reverse | Build **early** return buffer; last coaches thin out |

Municipal tourism lists **Hadong / Hwagae / Jingyo** terminal pages under traffic guides — open those the morning you move rather than freezing one PDF timetable here.

## Hourly outline (tea-forward day)

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| Morning | Arrive Hadong; taxi/bus toward Hwagae |
| Late morning | Tea Cultural Center / museum block (check Monday closure) |
| Midday | Hwagae Market walk + simple lunch via open pins |
| Afternoon | Short tea-field viewpoint walk **or** return toward Seomjin pine edge if energy remains |
| Late afternoon | Begin return toward Hadong lodging or Jinju base before dark |

## Food method (no invented “best matcha cafe” list)
Hadong municipal pages list **food categories** (green-tea cold noodles, river eel, marsh clam soups, etc.) without forcing one shop name into this guide.

| Zone | How to choose |
|---|---|
| Hwagae Market lanes | High turnover; verify open shutters day-of |
| Hadong-eup | Denser indoor options if you base in town |
| Roadside rest | Only as backup; not a cuisine plan |

If a cafe inside the tea center is open, treat it as **on-site convenience**, not a ranked review.

## Hub stay honesty
- **Hadong lodging works** → sleep here; roam Hwagae and river edges.
- **Thin dates / full inns** → **Jinju** is the practical lodging-rich fallback, then day-bus in.
- **Busan same-day** is possible only as a long grind — we do **not** recommend it as the default frame.

## Manners
- Tea fields are working landscapes — stay on public paths; no trampling rows for photos.
- Jirisan fringe weather flips fast; pack a shell even on “clear” apps.
- **1330** for English help; municipal tourist lines above for Korean-first detail.
