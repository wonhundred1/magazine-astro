**Hub:** Busan · **Car:** no · **Hotel:** Busan stay · **Mode:** ticketed evening (or weekend daytime)

Unlike the **free Haeundae Light Festival**, Christmas Village Busan is a **paid outdoor market** staged at the **Busan Cinema Center** plaza. Book before you romanticize a walk-up entry on peak nights.

## Who it is for
- Travelers who want a **structured Christmas market** (stalls, staged winter set pieces)
- Visitors okay with **advance tickets** and bag checks typical of ticketed grounds
- People based near Line 2 / Centum who can keep the night short
