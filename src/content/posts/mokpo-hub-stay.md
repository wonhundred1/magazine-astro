**Hub stay: Mokpo** · **Car:** no · **Airport note:** Muan (MWX) → ground transfer into Mokpo · **Not:** Busan same-day sightseeing

Mokpo is a **lodging-rich port city** on the west Jeolla coast. Stay here and move nearby. Do not burn a full day on a rushed Busan round trip just to “check” the harbor.

## Who it is for
- Travelers entering west Jeolla by KTX/SRT or express coach
- People building **Jindo / Haenam / island** legs from a stable hotel base
- Gyopo and Korean-map users who can run city buses in Kakao/Naver

## Why Mokpo is the hub (not a satellite)
City tourism materials treat Mokpo as a **walkable cluster**: Yudalsan, modern-history streets, North Port cable, Gatbawi sea walk, markets. Hotels and stations sit inside that cluster. That is the opposite of a thin-lodging satellite that forces you back to another metro every night.

## Arrival table (patterns — reconfirm same day)

| Leg | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Seoul area → **Mokpo Station** | KTX/SRT class | Public course examples often land in a **~2–2.5 h** band from Yongsan/Suseo-class gates — **board times change** |
| Seoul area → **Mokpo Bus Terminal** | Express / intercity coach | Multi-hour; use terminal inquiry boards the morning you go |
| **MWX** (Muan) → Mokpo | Airport bus / taxi | Build buffer; you do not walk out of the terminal into the harbor |
| Station or terminal → hotel | Taxi / city bus | Save the hotel pin the night before |
| Busan → Mokpo | Long coach or rail | Treat as a **relocation day**, not a sightseeing day |
| Mokpo → Jindo / Haenam | Day coach / local links | Return to the **Mokpo bed** unless you planned an honest overnight |

## What to do from this hub (index)
1. **In-city half-days:** Yudalsan + modern history street; marine cable to Gohado; Gatbawi + skywalk evening (separate deep pieces on this site).
2. **Official day-tour skeleton:** Mokpo city tourism lists a daytime city-tour window around **09:30–15:30** with stops that match the walkable spine (Sihwa alley, skywalk, Yudalsan, modern history halls, Gatbawi, Nobel Peace museum, fish market, North Port cable). Use it as a **map of places**, even if you self-guide.
3. **Day radius:** Jindo, Haenam Usuyeong — return to Mokpo unless lodging is intentionally elsewhere.
4. **Ferries / weather holds:** only with same-day timetable proof; west-coast wind cancels plans without apology.

## Soft first-day hourly outline

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| Morning | Arrive station/terminal; hotel drop; short rest |
| Late morning | One low-stress walk block (history street **or** Yudalsan foot of the hill) |
| Lunch | Map method near old downtown / market edges |
| Afternoon | Either cable–Gohado **or** rest + laundry — do not stack both with jet lag |
| Evening | Harbor lights or Gatbawi walk if wind is kind; early sleep |

## Food method (no invented shops)
We do **not** invent bakery rankings or “must” restaurants here.

| Zone | How to choose |
|---|---|
| Old downtown / Sihwa edges | High turnover; save 2–3 open places day-of |
| Fish market belt | Good for simple bowls; check closing hours early |
| Hotel corridor | Indoor fallback when wind is harsh |

Call ahead only when language allows. Freeze no ranking.

## Manners
- Coastal wind and stairs on Yudalsan — layers, non-slip shoes.
- National-park manners on island legs later in the trip.
- Korea Travel Hotline **1330** for English help.
