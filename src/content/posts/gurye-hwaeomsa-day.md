**Hub stay: Suncheon or Gwangju** (lodging-rich) · **Car:** no · **Return:** hub bed · **Not:** Busan same-day stack with Hadong

Hwaeomsa is the **Gurye / Jirisan** classic: National Treasure halls, stone lanterns, forest approach. VisitKorea lists open **00:00–18:00** with **admission prohibited after sunset**, inquiry **+82-61-783-7600**, address **539 Hwaeomsa-ro, Masan-myeon, Gurye**. County fare tables list local **Hwaeomsa** adult band often **~₩1,700** class — **reconfirm ticket window** (policies shift).

## Who it is for
- Temple walkers already based in **Suncheon** or **Gwangju**
- People who accept **thin rural buses** and will screenshot the return clock
- Visitors pairing Seomjin / Hadong on a **different** day

## Overnight honesty
- Prefer **Suncheon** or **Gwangju** beds.
- Gurye overnight only with a real booking if you want dawn temple air.
- Busan same-day + Hwaeomsa + Hadong is a fatigue fail.

## Manners
- Temple silence zones; modest dress for halls.
- No smoking on paths; stay off restricted slopes.
