**Hub stay: Yeosu** · **Car:** no · **Return:** Yeosu bed after sunrise · **Not:** Busan same-night

Hyangiram means a hermitage **facing the sun**. VisitKorea lists the **Yeosu Hyangiram Sunrise Festival** **2026-12-31 ~ 2027-01-01** at **Hyangiram Sunrise Park & Hermitage**, host Yeosu-si, inquiry **+82-61-659-4743**, performance window class often **20:00–10:00**. Pair daylight [Odongdo](/posts/yeosu-odongdo/) on another date.

## Who it is for
- Travelers who booked **Yeosu** lodging for year-end
- Walkers OK with stairs, wind, and pre-dawn crowds on Dolsan
- People who will reconfirm shuttle/bus add-ons the week of Dec 31
