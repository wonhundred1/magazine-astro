# Quality bar (20/day target)

## Cadence (KST)
- **09:00** — publish/finalize up to 10 quality pieces
- **21:00** — publish/finalize up to 10 quality pieces
- Prefer skip over thin filler.

## Pre-work
Research, outlines, tables, cafe/map checks, and AI illustrations **may start before** 09:00 / 21:00.
Treat those hours as checkpoints for publish-ready output, not the first moment work can begin.

## Region
See `REGION_BALANCE.md`: Jeolla + Gyeongsang first; nationwide if the brief is strong; rotate cities; famous sites may repeat with new angles.

## Each piece must include
1. Long-form sections (who / transit table / hourly table / stops / food / manners / sources)
2. Rebuilt tables (never leave source table screenshots as final)
3. Multiple maps (destination + hub + food cluster)
4. Real cafes/restaurants with name + area; unknown shops -> place-sales list
5. Illustration-style image (AI), composition shifted from photo refs; caption discloses AI
6. EN = natural tone, not literal KO mirror
7. No invented businesses or fake stats
8. **Footer recommendations**: previous published posts always listed at bottom (related/archive)
9. **Mobile images**: every illustration must stay readable on phones — CSS constrains img to max-width:100% and max-height:~42vh (contain). Do not rely on raw full-bleed PNG height. Tables must scroll horizontally on small screens.
