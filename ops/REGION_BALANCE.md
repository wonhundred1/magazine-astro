# Region balance (editorial)

## Priority
1. **Jeolla (전라)** and **Gyeongsang (경상)** first — densest fit for Busan/hub day trips and rural archive tone.
2. **Nationwide OK** when the angle is strong (clear transit day-trip, long-form tables/maps/cafes).
3. Do **not** stack one city/county for many batches in a row.

## Rotation heuristic (each 10-piece batch)
- Aim mix across provinces/cities (e.g. Geoje + Tongyeong + Jeonju + Yeosu + Gyeongju — not five Geoje variants only).
- Famous sites **may repeat** with a new angle (season, transit variant, food cluster, rainy-day, family vs solo).
- When repeating a site, link the earlier piece in body and rely on the **bottom related/archive list**.

## Bottom recommendations (required)
- Every post footer shows previously published guides (same region preferred, then tags, then newest).
- Astro: automatic in `posts/[slug].astro`.
- WordPress: related block appended/updated on publish so older posts stay linked.
