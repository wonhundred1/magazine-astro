# Allowlisted sources (copyright-safe)

## Preferred
- Official government / city tourism pages (e.g. tour.geoje.go.kr)
- Korea Tourism Organization (visitkorea.or.kr) public pages
- Digital cultural encyclopedias and clearly public datasets
- Public transport operator notices when terms allow reuse with attribution

## Not allowed for auto-ingest
- Full-text scrape of blogs, newspapers, or magazines
- User photos / UGC without license
- Paid guidebooks or closed APIs without license
- Copy-paste of third-party articles with slight rewrite

## Rules
1. Record URL + access date in frontmatter sources and lastVerified.
2. Paraphrase facts; do not mirror long quotes or structure.
3. Prefer linking out over republishing.
4. Cadence: morning transport/hours; evening outdoor/weather-sensitive notes.
